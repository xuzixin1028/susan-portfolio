- The website should NOT vertically scroll on desktop.
- Everything should fit within one browser viewport (100vh).
- The main visual concept is a physical cork pinboard / creative workspace.
- Use the attached reference image as visual inspiration for the overall composition, layering, pinned-paper aesthetic, shadows, cork texture, and playful editorial design.
- Do not directly copy the reference. Adapt the visual language into a unique portfolio for Susan Xu.
- The design should feel creative and playful but still polished enough for product management, UX/product design, and technology recruiting.

Create a warm corkboard background covering the entire viewport.

Use:
- warm tan/brown cork texture
- subtle grain and small cork imperfections
- realistic push pins
- paper cards
- sticky notes
- index cards
- taped or pinned project cards
- soft realistic shadows
- slight rotations (1–3 degrees) on some objects
- layered objects that make the page feel physically assembled

Color palette:
- warm cork brown
- pale yellow
- cream / off-white
- black / charcoal
- small accents of red, blue, orange, and dark green

Typography:
Use a combination of:
1. clean modern sans-serif typography for major headings
2. monospace/typewriter-inspired typography for labels, numbers, and metadata

The overall aesthetic should feel like:
editorial design + product designer portfolio + physical inspiration board.

INTERACTIONS:

The page should feel interactive even though it does not scroll.

When hovering over a project card:
- slightly lift the card
- increase its shadow
- straighten its rotation slightly
- show a subtle “View Project →” indicator

When clicking a project:
Open the project details without navigating away from the pinboard.

Use either:
- an expanding paper card
OR
- a large pinned-paper modal overlay

The project detail view should include placeholders for:

Include an X or “Back to Board” control to return to the pinboard.
